Service description

Soapbox Caddie provides laundry pickup and delivery in the River Region. Services include wash & fold, hang-dry care, and delicates handling.

Pricing and billing

One-time service is charged by the bag at the price shown when you book: $45 small, $60 medium, $80 large. Each bag has a fill line printed on it; the price covers a bag filled to that line.

The subscription carries a one-time $75 startup fee and is billed at $55 per week, which includes two bags per week. Each additional bag in a given week is $25. You may pause, skip or cancel at any time.

Pickup and delivery

You schedule a 2-hour pickup window. We collect your bags during that window. Delivery dates are capacity-driven rather than fixed at booking, and are communicated to you by SMS. We do not guarantee a specific delivery date at the time of booking.

Contactless pickup

You may leave bags in a safe spot (front door, lobby, gate). Soapbox Caddie is not responsible for loss or theft of items left unattended. We are not responsible for damage to items not disclosed as delicate at booking.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refunds for partial billing periods.

Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, Soapbox Caddie's liability for any lost or damaged item is limited to the cost of that item up to $50 per bag. We are not liable for dry-clean-only or undisclosed special-care items.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will notify you by email at least 14 days before material changes take effect.
