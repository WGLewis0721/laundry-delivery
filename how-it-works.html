How Soapbox Caddie works

From your front door to clean folded clothes and back — without a trip to the laundromat. Three steps. Zero hassle.

Step 1

Book & get your bag

Choose a one-time service or the weekly subscription, and pick your bag size — small, medium or large. Your Soapbox Caddie bag arrives within a few days.

Every bag has a fill line printed on it. Fill to that line and the price is the price — no counting items, no weighing, no surprise charges.

Step 2

Schedule a pickup

Pick a 2-hour window online — up to seven days ahead. We work around your schedule, not the other way around.

Leave your filled Soapbox Caddie bag by the door, in a lobby, or at a gate. Add a note for any special instructions ("front porch, left of door · gate code #1234"). Contactless pickup — no need to be home.

Schedule a pickup
Step 3

We clean & return

Your laundry is washed, dried and folded with care, then returned to your door. You are charged for the bag you booked — nothing is added quietly.

We wash and fold everything according to your care instructions, then set a delivery date based on this week's capacity. You'll get a text when it's confirmed, and another when it's at your door.
