# Responsive structural wireframes

15 live routes × desktop (1100×830) and mobile (420×990) = **30 actual SVG artifacts**, plus PNG raster copies. [Screen map](../SCREEN_MAP.md) links to every page/spec. [Blueprint source](../src/page-blueprints.json) preserves route headings, key sections, primary CTA, important states.

The SVGs are intentionally simplified wireframes **based on inspected source**, not actual browser screenshots or final approved redesigns. The SVG is the editable original. PNGs in [exports/](exports/) are generated by `python scripts/design/render-png.py`.
