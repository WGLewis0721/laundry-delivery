### Rules
1. Do not fabricate a logo or assert that the rendered typographic title is the authorized logo.
2. Keep original source imagery, photo cropping, photography rights and object positioning in an asset provenance inventory.
3. Maintain the six current palette colors and their semantic surface/text roles.
4. Respect documented font substitutions: OKANA/ENRIQ require authentic licensed sources before implementation.
5. Use the [icon set](ICONOGRAPHY.md) consistently; avoid mixing unrelated stroke styles.
6. Review desktop/mobile before altering approved marketing composition.
