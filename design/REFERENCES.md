## Brand authority and rights

Client-signed brand/artwork approvals and legitimate font rights take precedence over examples. The deployed Soapbox Caddie source remains the default visual baseline until changed through an approved request. No source in this register independently establishes rights for OKANA/ENRIQ, logo masters, photographs or customer likenesses.
