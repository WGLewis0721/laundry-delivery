How bag pricing works

Every Soapbox Caddie bag has a fill line printed on it. Fill the bag to that line and the price on this page is the price you pay.

  • Pick your bag size — small, medium or large. The bag you choose sets the price.
  • Fill it to the line — no sorting, no counting items, no weighing.
  • Need more room? — book a second bag, or move to the subscription where extra bags are $25 each.
